# Temperature transfer across datasets (T per type only)

Answers: Typed Decisions `results-repeat/train` (train) and `results-repeat/test` (test), the rest `results-repeat/test`; passes: pass1. Rows: where the temperatures were fitted. Columns: where they are scored. `raw` is T = 1; the diagonal (bold) is the in-domain fit (Typed Decision Bench: 5-fold cross-fit); `pooled others` is one fit on every source except the column's dataset, `pooled text` and `pooled images` the same over text or image sources only. Sets without answers in these roots are left out. Generated by `transfer.py`. Quick directional run: new image sets are the first 300 cases of each split, one pass (`results-vision-quick/`); CIFAR-10H and POPE are pass 1 of `results-vision/`. Command: `transfer.py "$DATASETS_DIR" --td-train results-repeat/train --td-test results-repeat/test --real results-repeat/test --vision <dir linking results-vision/<config>/{cifar10h,pope}-* and results-vision-quick/<config>/*> --passes pass1`.
